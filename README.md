# Free the Stack

### Rebuild the apps. Give people control.

Free the Stack is a collection of free, community-built alternatives to the apps we depend on. We study how useful software works, rebuild the experiences we love, and share what we learn so anyone can use, understand, and improve it.

Software should give people freedom: to choose, to customize, to leave, and to own their data.

## What we're building

- Independent alternatives to everyday apps.
- Transparent implementations you can inspect and run yourself.
- Practical breakdowns of how apps work and how to recreate their features.
- Projects built in public, with room for anyone to contribute.

## Our principles

- **People first.** Build around users and their needs.
- **Less lock-in.** Favor portable data, open formats, and clear export paths.
- **Learn by rebuilding.** Turn curiosity into working software and useful explanations.
- **Share the work.** Make projects accessible and document how to get started.
- **Build independently.** Use original implementations and assets, and respect third-party licenses.

## Get involved

Have an app you'd love to see reimagined? [Open an issue](https://github.com/Nikhil12108/Free-the-Stack/issues) and tell us:

1. Which app or feature you have in mind.
2. What you like about it.
3. What you'd change or make freely available.

You can also help with code, design, testing, documentation, and ideas. Before starting a large contribution, open an issue so we can agree on its scope.
